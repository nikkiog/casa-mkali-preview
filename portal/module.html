02Do's and don'tsthe non-negotiables

always

  • speak from your own experience
  • tag the brand and mark #ad on everything
  • keep reels tight: 20 to 40 seconds
  • use the phrase library when in doubt

never

  • no cure, treat, or heal claims of any kind
  • no mentions of other brands or family ventures
  • no unapproved discounts or promises
  • when unsure, ask before you post, not after
