cookies.

What we track, why, and how to say no.

01The short versionNo surprises

we count visits,
not people.

We use one analytics tool — Google Analytics — to see which pages get read and where visitors come from, so we can make the site better. It only runs if you click accept on the cookie banner.

No advertising trackers, no selling data, no cross-site profiling. If you decline, the only thing stored is your "no" — so we don't ask again.

03Your choicesYou're in control

change your mind
any time.

Accept or decline on the banner when you first visit. To change your choice later, reset your cookie choice — the banner will come back and ask again.

You can also clear or block cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's own opt-out browser add-on.
